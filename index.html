How Feetzer works

One request. Relevant local people.

Feetzer is a temporary request network—not an endless content feed. Requests are shared with relevant people nearby and can disappear when no longer useful.

1

Describe the moment

Ask a question, request small help, or arrange a suitable local delivery with place and timing.

2

Receive useful responses

Nearby people can add first-hand local context or practical help. AI may assist when relevant information is available online.

3

Choose what helps

Use the answer, continue a conversation, accept help, arrange the handoff, or discover somewhere worth visiting.

AI is assistance—not the promise.

If a question has useful online or structured information, AI can offer a quick starting point. When the answer depends on what is happening now, personal experience, or action in the physical world, nearby people add what AI cannot reliably provide.
