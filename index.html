How Feetzer works

One request. The right local context.

Feetzer is a temporary request network—not an endless feed. A request reaches relevant people nearby and disappears when it is no longer useful.

1. Ask for what you need

Post a question, recommendation, help request, or small delivery with your location and timing.

2. Get useful signals

Feetzer may provide an AI-assisted answer when relevant information is available, while nearby people can add first-hand context or practical help.

3. Choose the response

Continue with the answer, chat with a suitable person, accept help, arrange a delivery, or discover a local gem.

AI can help when information exists

For questions with useful online or structured information, AI may provide a quick starting point. It is assistance—not a replacement for local presence.

People matter when context or action is needed

A nearby person can share what is true right now, explain the local nuance, recommend from experience, or help in the physical world.

What people can do

Ask. Help. Deliver. Discover.

Feetzer is for the small moments that fall between search, social media, and standard delivery apps.

Ask

Help

Deliver

Discover

Find hidden places and neighborhood businesses through people who know the area.

Built for trust

Local does not have to mean random.

Feetzer is designed around relevant matching, user verification, credibility, ratings, controlled chat, and delivery tracking. The goal is to make nearby human help easier to approach responsibly.
