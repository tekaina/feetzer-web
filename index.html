How every Feetzer moment works

  1. 01A need appears — post a short, time-limited request.
  2. 02Seen nearby — shared around that spot, not a stranger directory.
  3. 03Voluntary replies — locals answer if they know or want to help.
  4. 04You decide next — use the tip, chat, or close anytime.

How it works

Three calm steps. No directory of strangers.

On Feetzer, you never cold-message people from a public map of profiles. Every interaction starts with a clear, temporary local need.

1

Share what you need

Write a short question or request, pin the relevant place or street corner, and set how long it stays active—from a quick 15-minute check up to 3 hours.

2

People nearby can respond

Feetzer users in the area can see your active request and choose to reply. When useful public information exists online, an AI-assisted note can offer a quick starting point too.

3

Chat and decide what’s next

Read the answers, continue in chat if you want to coordinate with someone helpful, or close the request as soon as you have what you need.

✦
Good to know: Replies are always voluntary and local availability varies by neighborhood and time of day. Feetzer connects neighbors rather than automatically dispatching guaranteed service providers.

More than another feed

Less scrolling. More local life.

Most apps compete to keep your eyes on a screen. Feetzer is built to help you get a useful local answer, meet up, or solve something small—and get back to your day.

Time-limited by design

You choose an active time window when posting (up to 3 hours). Once that window ends or you close the request, it leaves the live nearby feed instead of piling up in an endless public timeline.

Purpose first, not follower counts

There are no public popularity feeds to perform for and no open directory of nearby strangers to browse. Conversations start around a concrete request or local activity.

Trust & control

Clear boundaries you stay in charge of.

Connecting with people nearby works best when you know what is shared, how profiles are presented, and what controls are always within reach.

Optional ID verification badge

Members can submit an identity document for moderation review to display a verified badge on their profile. A badge indicates reviewed account identity—it is not a criminal background check, skill certification, or guarantee of safety.

In-app reporting & blocking

You can block another user immediately or report profiles, requests, and chat messages from any interaction screen. Reports are reviewed by Feetzer moderation.

Foreground location & settings

Device GPS is read only while the app is open—never tracked in the background—and can be revoked anytime in system settings. When posting a request, pin a public spot or street corner rather than a private address.

Practical answers

Frequently asked questions

What is Feetzer? +

Feetzer is a mobile app that connects everyday local questions, small practical needs, place discovery, and casual neighborhood activities with people who are actually nearby.

What can I ask for? +

Everyday local questions (like current queues, quiet tables, or neighborhood tips), small practical favors, place recommendations, or company for a casual local activity. Feetzer is not for emergencies, medical or security situations, or regulated commercial services.

Who sees my request? +

Active requests are shown to Feetzer users near the location you pin while your chosen time window is open. Requests are not published to the open web, and Feetzer does not let people browse a directory of nearby strangers.

Can I answer or help too? +

Yes. Every Feetzer account can both post requests and reply to nearby ones—sharing a quick local tip, answering a question on your street, or joining and hosting community activities.

Can I use it outside Vienna? +

Yes. Feetzer is built for neighborhoods anywhere, though Vienna is where it began. Because replies come from real people nearby, activity depends on how many locals already use Feetzer in your area—inviting a few neighbors or friends is a great way to start locally.

Is downloading and using it free? +

Yes—downloading Feetzer from Google Play and using its live features (posting requests, replying, discovering nearby places, and joining activities) is currently free. While our Terms of Service describe potential optional paid features in the future, paid transactions and subscriptions are not currently active.

What if nobody replies? +

Replies are voluntary and never guaranteed. If nobody nearby responds before your timer runs out, your request automatically leaves the live feed and moves to your personal expired history, where you can repost it later if still useful. When relevant public place information exists, Feetzer may also show an AI-assisted starting point.

How do I report a problem? +

Inside the app, tap the three-dot menu on any profile, request, or chat screen to report content or block a user immediately. You can also reach our team directly by email at feetzer.io@gmail.com.
